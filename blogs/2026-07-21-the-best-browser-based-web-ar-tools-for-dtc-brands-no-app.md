In 2026, direct-to-consumer (DTC) brands face a retail market where shoppers demand interactive, physical-world verification before purchasing. Modern consumers expect to rotate products, configure materials, and place them directly into their physical space through an augmented reality website, rather than relying on flat 2D photography. Historically, achieving this level of interactivity required shoppers to download a dedicated mobile application.
