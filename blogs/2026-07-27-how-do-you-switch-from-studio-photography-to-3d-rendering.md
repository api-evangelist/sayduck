Short answer: You can add 3D and app-less AR to BigCommerce without code by installing an app from the BigCommerce App Marketplace, uploading your 3D models (GLB/USDZ), and dragging the 3D widget into Page Builder. Setup takes under an hour if you already have 3D models. The transition from 2D photography to interactive 3D product visualization represents a major structural shift in online retail.
